## Blocking findings

These must be resolved before promotion. They cannot be accepted as residual risk.

### `HW-002` — workflow · field `steps`

no reachable step ends the workflow — every reachable step leads somewhere else, so the work never terminates

**Fix:** Give the final step an empty next_steps list, or add the step that completes the work.

### `HW-003` — step `publish-post` · field `actor`

step 'publish-post' has no actor — nobody is named as performing it

**Fix:** Name the person, role, or system that performs this step.

### `HW-004` — step `publish-post` · field `decision_authority`

step 'publish-post' makes a decision but names no decision authority

**Fix:** Name the specific person or role with authority to decide here.

### `HW-005` — step `publish-post` · field `next_steps`

step 'publish-post' points to 'promote-post', which is not a declared step

**Fix:** Add the missing step, or correct the reference to an existing one.

### `HW-006` — step `archive-notes` · field `next_steps`

step 'archive-notes' cannot be reached from the entry step — nothing routes to it

**Fix:** Route to this step from where it really happens, or remove it.

### `HW-008` — step `scope-check` · field `outputs`

work passes from 'operator' at step 'scope-check' to 'research-helper' at step 'deep-research' with no verifiable artifact handed over

**Fix:** Give the upstream step an output with an artifact type and an evidence requirement, so the receiver knows what they are getting.

### `HW-009` — step `deep-research` · field `inputs.source`

**Fix:** Name the actor, system, or upstream step this input comes from.

### `HW-010` — step `request-artwork` · field `outputs.artifact_type`

**Fix:** State what kind of thing this output is.

### `HW-010` — step `request-artwork` · field `outputs.evidence_requirement`

**Fix:** State what must exist to prove this output was really produced.

### `HW-010` — step `scope-check` · field `outputs.artifact_type`

output 'scope decision' on step 'scope-check' has no artifact type — there is no saying what is produced

**Fix:** State what kind of thing this output is.

### `HW-010` — step `scope-check` · field `outputs.evidence_requirement`

### `HW-011` — workflow · field `observed_exceptions`

**Fix:** Add an exception path for this failure on the step where it occurs, with an owner and what is done.
